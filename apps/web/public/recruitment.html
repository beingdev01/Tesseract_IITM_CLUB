Foundation & Identity

What is Tesseract?

Tesseract is the official Esports Society of the IIT Madras BS Program, built around competitive esports, casual gaming, mind games, puzzles, strategy, and community. What started as a small gaming community has grown into a recognized society focused on creating an active ecosystem where students can compete, create, organize, collaborate, and have fun.

🎮

Competitive Esports

Competitive gaming across major multiplayer titles such as Free Fire, BGMI, Valorant, Call of Duty: Mobile (CODM), Clash of Clans (COC), Minecraft, and other competitive PC/mobile titles.

Valorant BGMI Free Fire CODM COC
🕹️

Casual & Social Gaming

Fun and engaging games designed for recreation, social interaction, and high participation. Community game nights, friendly scrims, and lighthearted competitions.

Skribbl Among Us Smash Karts Subway Surfers GeoGuessr
🧩

Mind Games & Strategy

Puzzles, riddles, logical challenges, escape-room-style events, gaming quizzes, and strategy competitions that challenge participants to think, solve, and outsmart.

Escape Rooms Logic Puzzles Riddles Gaming Quizzes Tactical Games

GAME WING

The Game Wing is at the heart of Tesseract's competitive esports ecosystem. It is responsible for building communities around individual titles, organizing tournaments, and managing competitive matches.

You Don't Have to Be an Expert

Tesseract isn't looking only for professional gamers, experienced developers, seasoned designers, or veteran organizers. We are looking for students who want to build.

✦ Have the skills? Step into leadership as a Wing Head or Co-Head and run major initiatives.
✦ Starting out? Join as a Volunteer or Executive, learn from peers, and build real projects.
✦ Have an event idea? Pitch it, take ownership, and we will help you bring it to life across the BS community.

What matters most is your interest, initiative, willingness to learn, and willingness to take ownership.
